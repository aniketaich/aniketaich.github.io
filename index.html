About

Building practical solutions for complex systems

My superpower is my ability to work effectively with different groups of people and help them reach actionable solutions to complex problems. My expertise focuses on creating structures and models that promote healthier people, communities, and a healthier planet.

My background is in economics. I have worked as a field researcher, high school teacher, college instructor, management consultant, and program manager. My dream is to teach and farm. I hold a master’s degree from UC Berkeley.
