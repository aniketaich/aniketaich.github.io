About

Aniket Aich

I am a food-systems advocate with experience building programs that transform food systems and advance regenerative organic practices.

My superpower is my ability to work effectively with different groups of people and help them reach actionable solutions to complex problems. My work focuses on creating structures and models that promote healthier people, stronger communities, and a healthier planet.

I studied economics in college and have worked as a field researcher, high school teacher, college instructor, management consultant, and program manager. My dream is to teach and farm. I hold a master’s degree from UC Berkeley.
